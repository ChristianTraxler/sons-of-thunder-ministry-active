Our Ministry

Reaching Out & Serving Others in Christ's Name

Welcome to Sons of Thunder Ministry! If you're a first-time visitor, we welcome you in the name of Jesus. Our ministry reaches communities through motorsport chaplaincy, hospital outreach, and weekly scripture-based messages.

Each week, Pastor Hugh Compton — known as “Preacherbuddy” — provides messages on YouTube. Tune in Wednesdays at @hughcompton4575, and catch our broadcast on WHIP Radio 1350 at 10:00 AM.

We also offer Our Daily Bread devotionals free of charge to anyone who requests one. Reach out and we'll make sure you receive it monthly.

We are called by God to reach out and serve others in Christ's name. It is through the guidance of the Holy Spirit that we humbly serve a God of love.

What We Do

Our Outreach & Goals for 2024

🏁

Chariots for Jesus

Charlotte Motor Speedway Summer Shootout chaplaincy ministry — bringing the gospel to the racing community.

☁

Fuel for the Soul

Weekly spiritual messages and devotionals designed to sustain and encourage believers through the week.

❤

Shriners Hospital

Ministry and pastoral care outreach to families and patients at Shriners Hospital, bringing comfort and hope.

🤝

UMAR Outreach

Partnership with UMAR to serve and uplift individuals with developmental disabilities in our community.

🏎

Racing Chaplain

Serving as team chaplain for the Carolina Pro Late Model Series (CPLMS) and the CCMSS racing communities.

⛪

Vanderburg Methodist

Serving as minister at Vanderburg Methodist in Mooresville, NC — watch sermons at YouTube @Vanderburg1809.
